# Code of Conduct

We want participation in NEXUS to be respectful and constructive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussion and review.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person’s private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable in project spaces or when someone is representing the project.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where a person is acting on behalf of NEXUS. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through GitHub, if a private contact option is available. Otherwise, open an issue asking for a private reporting channel without including names or sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion. Depending on severity, maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation.

For security vulnerabilities, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting details publicly.
